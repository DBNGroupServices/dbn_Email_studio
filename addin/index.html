DBN Email Studio

Outlook add-in — install

  1. Download the add-in manifest: manifest.xml
  2. In Outlook Classic: Get Add-ins → My add-ins → Custom add-ins → Add a custom add-in → Add from file, choose the file, and confirm.
  3. Restart Outlook, open any email and click Email Studio on the Home tab.
  4. In the pane: Settings (gear) → AI connection, paste your Claude API key and press Save & test.

No desktop app is needed. If your organisation blocks custom add-ins, give manifest.xml to IT to deploy.

Before you write

  • Pane stuck or blank: close the pane and reopen it. If a message appears, include a screenshot of it.
  • Sign-in problems: make sure you are signed in to Outlook with your Microsoft 365 work or personal account, then choose Settings → Refresh.
  • Paid but still on the trial: choose Settings → Refresh. It can take a minute after checkout.
  • Billing, invoices, cancelling or changing plan: Settings → Manage billing.
  • Team seats: colleagues in your Microsoft 365 organisation get a seat when they first sign in, until all seats are used. The Team owner can add seats under Manage billing.

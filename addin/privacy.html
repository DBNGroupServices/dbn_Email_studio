Privacy

Draft for legal review. Not yet in effect.

Last updated 28 November 2026

Bulwark HQ ("we") provides Bulwark HQ Assistant, an add-in for Microsoft Outlook. This policy explains what we collect, why, and your choices. We handle personal information in line with the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles.

What we collect

  • Account: your Microsoft account ID, organisation (tenant) ID, name and email address, from Microsoft sign-in.
  • Subscription: plan, status and billing period. Payments are handled by Stripe; we never see or store your card number.
  • Usage counts: how many replies and suggestions you use each month, to apply plan limits.

Email content

When you ask for suggestions or a draft, the open email (and any attachments you choose) is sent over an encrypted connection to our service and on to Anthropic's Claude API to write the response. We do not store or log email content, drafts or attachments. Under Anthropic's commercial terms, API inputs and outputs are not used to train models. Your voices, templates and preferences are stored in your own Outlook mailbox settings, not on our servers.

Who we share with

  • Microsoft (sign-in), Anthropic (AI drafting), Stripe (payments) and Cloudflare (hosting). Some of these process data outside Australia, including in the United States.
  • We don't sell personal information or use it for advertising.

Keeping and deleting

We keep account and billing records while you have an account and as required for tax law. To access, correct or delete your information, or to complain about how we've handled it, email privacy@bulwarkhq.com.au. If you're not satisfied with our response, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (oaic.gov.au).

How the free trial works

Sign in with your Microsoft account from the Assistant pane in Outlook. Your trial runs until the end of your third working day (Monday to Friday, Melbourne time) or 50 replies, whichever comes first, using our premium model. When it ends, choose a plan in the pane. Secure checkout is by Stripe. Prices are in Australian dollars and include GST. Cancel any time from Settings → Manage billing; your plan runs to the end of the period you paid for.

Your email stays yours

The open email is sent to the AI service only to write your draft. We don't store or log email content, and nothing is ever sent from your mailbox automatically: every draft opens in Outlook for you to review. See our privacy policy.

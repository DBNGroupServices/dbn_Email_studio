DBN Email Studio — quick help

Reply in three clicks

  1. Open an email and click Email Studio on the Home ribbon. Pin the pane (pin icon at the top of the pane) to keep it open as you move between emails.
  2. Click a quick reply (Acknowledge, Confirm, Ask for info…) or type a few dot points and press Ctrl+Enter.
  3. Check the draft and the before you send notes, then choose Open reply or Reply all. Nothing is ever sent automatically.

Make it sound like you

Settings → Voice profiles. Create one per audience (tenants, contractors, executives, team). Set formality, warmth and directness, your greeting and sign-off, things to avoid, and paste up to three emails you've written — Claude copies their rhythm and wording. Choose a Default voice so every reply starts there.

Tip: open one of your own sent emails, then use + Use the open email as an example.

Refine without retyping

Use Shorter, Warmer, Firmer, More formal, Simpler, Bullets or Polish — or type your own change. Use ‹ › to step back to an earlier version. Choose More controls → Options to compare to get two or three different drafts at once.

Safety checks

Before you open a reply, the pane flags unfilled placeholders such as [confirm date], mentioned-but-missing attachments, commitments (refunds, guarantees, liability) and deadlines. With the Security disclosure guard on (default), it also blocks access/alarm codes, PINs, passwords, card numbers and security-coverage detail from drafts. Red items need a second click to proceed.

Connecting Claude

  1. Sign in at console.anthropic.com, add credit under Billing, then create a key under API keys.
  2. In the pane, open Settings (gear) → AI connection, paste the key, choose a model and press Save & test.

Claude Opus 5.5 writes best; Sonnet 5.5 is faster and about half the cost; Haiku 5.5 is the cheapest. A typical reply costs a few cents or less.

If something goes wrong

  • "Sorry, we can't load the add-in" — the pane is a web page at this site. Check you're online and that the site opens in Edge; if your network blocks it, ask IT to allow dbngroupservices.github.io.
  • "Could not reach api.anthropic.com" — your network may block it. Ask IT to allow api.anthropic.com (HTTPS).
  • "No API credit" — add credit at console.anthropic.com → Billing.
  • Pane looks out of date — close Outlook, delete %LOCALAPPDATA%\Microsoft\Office\16.0\Wef, reopen Outlook.

Privacy

There is no desktop app and no DBN server. The pane reads only the email you have open. When you press Generate, the email text, your notes and any files you add go directly from Outlook to Anthropic's API using your key. Your key, settings, voice profiles and templates are saved in your Outlook mailbox's add-in settings, and only this add-in can read them. Under Anthropic's commercial terms, API inputs are not used to train models.

DBN EMAIL STUDIO 2.0
Your everyday email writing assistant

OUTLOOK ADD-IN (recommended)
1. Open the "Outlook add-in" folder and double-click "Install Email Studio.cmd".
   Choose Yes when Windows asks to install the DBN Email Studio Local CA certificate.
2. In Outlook: Get Add-ins > My add-ins > Custom add-ins > Add from file,
   then pick manifest.xml in %LOCALAPPDATA%\Programs\DBN Email Studio
   (first install, or once when upgrading from 1.x).
3. Open an email and click "Email Studio" on the Home tab.
If Outlook ever says "Sorry, we can't load the add-in", double-click
"Fix Email Studio.cmd".

DESKTOP APP

1. Open "DBN Email Studio.exe".
2. Open Settings and leave "My existing ChatGPT plan" selected.
3. Choose "Continue with ChatGPT", sign in on OpenAI's website, and allow
   "Use your ChatGPT plan". Return to Email Studio when sign-in completes.
4. Add your signature and preferred writing style once.

Then paste an email, drop in a screenshot or saved email, or add dot points.
Choose Reply, Rewrite or From dot points, and create your draft.
Edit it, ask for changes, then copy the final email into Outlook.

OUTLOOK CLASSIC SHORTCUTS
Open Outlook Classic, then click "Import from Outlook Classic".
Browse folders or search subject/sender, select an email, then press
"Import selected email". Cancel leaves your work in place.
After editing your draft, use "Open reply in Outlook Classic" to review it there.
The app does not send emails automatically.

CONNECTION TROUBLE
Use Continue with ChatGPT again if your session needs reconnecting.
Manage usage opens your ChatGPT plan and app limits.
Test drafting uses fictional text and a small amount of your selected allowance.
The app never automatically switches to separate API billing.

The app runs independently of ChatGPT and Codex. No Python installation needed.
AI drafting needs internet access and an eligible, connected ChatGPT plan.
Separate API billing is an optional connection in Settings.

Your saved sign-in, preferences and drafts are encrypted for your Windows account.
Your source material is sent to OpenAI when you create or refine a draft.
The app does not send emails. Review the draft before using it.

To fully close the app: Settings > Quit app.
For details, supported files and troubleshooting, see README.md.

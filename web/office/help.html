DBN Email Studio

This personal Outlook add-in uses the local DBN Email Studio application on this computer.

Keep the desktop engine running. Connect your ChatGPT plan in the desktop app. Use the ribbon button while reading or composing an email.

Read and compose access applies to the current item. The add-in does not send email automatically. Broad mailbox automation and meeting services are separate planned stages.

For connection issues, reopen the desktop application and review Settings. If your organisation blocks custom add-ins, ask your Microsoft 365 administrator.

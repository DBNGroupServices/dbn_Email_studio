DBN Email Studio — quick help

Reply in three clicks

  1. Open an email and click Email Studio on the Home ribbon. Pin the pane (pin icon at the top of the pane) to keep it open as you move between emails.
  2. Click a quick reply (Acknowledge, Confirm, Ask for info…) or type a few dot points and press Ctrl+Enter.
  3. Check the draft and the before you send notes, then choose Open reply or Reply all. Nothing is ever sent automatically.

Make it sound like you

Settings → Voice profiles. Create one per audience (tenants, contractors, executives, team). Set formality, warmth and directness, your greeting and sign-off, things to avoid, and paste up to three emails you've written — the AI copies their rhythm and wording. Choose a Default voice so every reply starts there.

Tip: open one of your own sent emails, then use + Use the open email as an example.

Refine without retyping

Use Shorter, Warmer, Firmer, More formal, Simpler, Bullets or Polish — or type your own change. Use ‹ › to step back to an earlier version. Choose More controls → Options to compare to get two or three different drafts at once.

Safety checks

Before you open a reply, the pane flags unfilled placeholders such as [confirm date], mentioned-but-missing attachments, commitments (refunds, guarantees, liability) and deadlines. With the Security disclosure guard on (default), it also blocks access/alarm codes, PINs, passwords, card numbers and security-coverage detail from drafts. Red items need a second click to proceed.

"Sorry, we can't load the add-in"

Outlook shows this when the Email Studio engine on your computer is not running or its certificate is not trusted. To fix:

  1. Open DBN Email Studio from the Start menu, then click Retry in Outlook.
  2. Still failing? Run Fix Email Studio.cmd in %LOCALAPPDATA%\Programs\DBN Email Studio (or the Outlook add-in folder of the release). It checks the engine, certificate, start-at-sign-in and Outlook's cache, and fixes what it can.
  3. Keep Start Email Studio when I sign in ticked (Settings → Reliability) so this doesn't happen after a restart.

Privacy

The pane only reads the email you have open. Email text, your notes and any files you add are sent to OpenAI only when you press Generate, using your connected ChatGPT plan (or your own API key). Settings, profiles and history are encrypted for your Windows account.
